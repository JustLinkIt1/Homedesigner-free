Delete your HomeDesigner account

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can delete your HomeDesigner account and the data stored with it at any time. This page explains how, what is removed, and what is kept. It applies to the Android app and to the web app.

Deleting is permanent. Your cloud designs, community posts and images, profile picture and AI points cannot be recovered afterwards.

How to delete your account

In the Android app

  1. Open HomeDesigner and go to Settings.
  2. Sign in with Google if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap the bin icon, Delete account, and confirm.

On the web

  1. Open homedesignerapp.com/app/ and go to Settings.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account.
  3. Select the bin icon, Delete account, and confirm.

You are signed out when it finishes.

By email

If you cannot use the app, email nathanjoppich@gmail.com from the Google account address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your designs saved in the cloud, and their deletion records.
  • Your community profile (handle, display name, bio), your threads and replies, and any reports you filed.
  • Your profile picture and the images you uploaded to the community.
  • Your AI points balance. Unused points are forfeited.
  • The link between your account and any render links we held (the result links are removed from our records).

What is kept

  • Pro bought on Google Play. The purchase stays with your Google Play account and can be restored in the app. Deleting your account does not refund or cancel it.
  • Pro bought on the web. Pro bought on the web stays linked to your Google account: deleting does not refund or cancel it, and signing in again with the same Google account brings it back.
  • Financial records. Records of point purchases and spending, and of Google Play purchases (purchase token, order ID, product, time), are kept because they are financial records. They are no longer linked to your account: your Google account identifier is replaced by a one-way hash, and the Play purchase record is unlinked.
  • Pseudonymous usage counts. We keep a hashed marker that your free AI render and free starting points were used, and a count of renders made, so the free offers cannot be claimed again by deleting and signing in again. These do not contain your name, email or Google identifier. They are pseudonymous rather than anonymous, because anyone who already knew your Google identifier could recompute the hash.
  • Anonymous usage events. The app's anonymous events are tied to a random install identifier, not to your account, so they cannot be removed per person. They are deleted automatically after 13 months.
  • Plans on your device. Plans saved on your phone or in your browser stay there. We cannot delete them for you. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them.
  • Records held by others. Google Play, Stripe, RevenueCat and our AI providers (ElevenLabs, fal.ai) keep their own records under their own privacy policies. AI renders are not deleted with your account: the images ElevenLabs or fal.ai generated, and the 3D views sent to make them, stay with those providers under their own terms (fal.ai: 90 days by default; ElevenLabs: no fixed period). We only remove our link.

See the Privacy Policy for the full picture, including retention and service providers.
