Floor plan creator

From a rough sketch to a plan you can hand to a builder

HomeDesigner is a floor plan app for homeowners, renovators and anyone arranging a room before buying furniture. The same plan opens on your phone and in the browser.

Draw walls that snap into place

Walls snap to the grid and to each other, so corners close cleanly. Rooms are detected as soon as the walls meet, with the area shown in square metres or square feet.

  • Exact measurements: type a length or drag a corner, in metric or imperial
  • Doors, windows and openings: in several styles, placed on any wall
  • Room names and areas: labelled automatically as you draw

Import a plan you already have

Bring in a PDF, a photo of a paper plan or a DXF file, set the scale with one known measurement, and trace over it. Then switch to 3D to check that the layout works.

  • PDF, image and DXF import: with automatic wall tracing for clean drawings
  • Several storeys: stack floors and connect them with stairs (Pro)
  • Export: PNG for free, PDF plan packs with areas and schedules in Pro

Questions

Good to know

Is the floor plan maker free?

Yes. You can draw, furnish and view plans in 3D for free, on Android and in the web app. HomeDesigner Pro adds several storeys, the complete catalog, PDF export, watermark-free images and unlimited saved projects.

Can I draw a floor plan to scale?

Yes. Every wall has a real length. Type the measurement you want, or drag and read the live dimension. Areas are calculated for each room.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes. The web app runs in the browser with nothing to install, and it is the same editor as the Android app.

Can I turn an existing plan into an editable one?

Yes. Import a PDF, image or DXF, set the scale from one measurement, and trace the walls. Clean drawings can be traced automatically.
