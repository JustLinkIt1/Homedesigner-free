Questions

Good to know

Is there an AI floor plan generator in HomeDesigner?

Yes. Open AI plan, choose the storeys, bedrooms, bathrooms and total size, say whether you want a garage and furniture, and describe the home in your own words. You get a floor plan with walls, doors and windows that opens as a normal project. Drawing it can take up to a minute.

Is the AI house plan generator free?

The app is free to download, and drawing, furnishing and importing a plan need no points. An AI house plan costs AI points. A new account with a verified Google sign-in gets free points to try it; after that, points are sold in packs in the app. Plans with two or three storeys need HomeDesigner Pro.

Can AI turn my own house map, PDF or photo into a design?

That is a different tool. The AI planner starts from your description, not from a file. To use a plan you already have, import a PDF, a photo or a scan: HomeDesigner detects the walls automatically, you set the scale with the real width of the plan, and it becomes 3D rooms. Clean black-and-white drawings work best, and you add doors, windows and furniture yourself.

Can I build from an AI-generated house plan?

Treat it as a first draft. The AI lays out rectangular rooms from your answers; you can ask it for changes or edit every wall yourself. It is not a construction drawing, so have an architect or engineer check anything you plan to build.

What do the AI tools cost?

They use points, which can be bought in the app. Everything else in HomeDesigner works without points.

Can I edit a plan the AI made?

Yes. The result is a normal HomeDesigner project. You can move walls, change rooms, swap furniture and view it in 3D.

How close is the AI render to my design?

It keeps the layout, walls and main furniture of your 3D view and adds realistic materials and lighting. It can add small decorative details, so treat it as an impression, not a technical drawing.

Where do my images go?

A render sends only the preview image to the rendering service. An AI plan sends your answers, and a designer review sends a picture of the plan. The privacy policy lists the services used.
