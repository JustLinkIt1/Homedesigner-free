Questions

Good to know

What do the AI tools cost?

They use points. New accounts get a one-time set of free points, enough to try an AI plan or a couple of renders, and more can be bought in the app. Everything else in HomeDesigner works without points.

Can I edit a plan the AI made?

Yes. The result is a normal HomeDesigner project. You can move walls, change rooms, swap furniture and view it in 3D.

How close is the AI render to my design?

It keeps the layout, walls and main furniture of your 3D view and adds realistic materials and lighting. It can add small decorative details, so treat it as an impression, not a technical drawing.

Where do my images go?

A render sends only the preview image to the rendering service. An AI plan sends your answers, and a designer review sends a picture of the plan. The privacy policy lists the services used.
