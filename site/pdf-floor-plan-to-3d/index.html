Walls detected automatically

Choose a PDF, PNG or JPG of your floor plan. HomeDesigner traces the walls, at any angle, not just straight up-and-down, and shows them over your plan before anything is placed.

  • Scanned or exported PDFs: both work, as do PNG and JPG images
  • Preview first: see every wall it found before you import
  • Adjust if needed: change the sensitivity for faint or busy plans

Rooms and scale, ready to edit

Set the real width of the plan and the dimensions come out to scale. Rooms are created from the walls automatically, so you can start moving walls, adding doors and windows, and furnishing right away.

  • To scale: set the plan width once, everything follows
  • Rooms built for you: from the walls that were found
  • Faint plan: keep it as a tracing background and draw over it

Then walk through it in 3D

After the import you are in the 2D editor. Switch to 3D, walk through the rooms, and try furniture to scale before anything is built or bought.

  • Walk-through 3D: on your phone or in the browser
  • Private tracing: the plan is traced in your browser; it is not sent to a server for tracing

Questions

Good to know

Is importing a PDF floor plan free?

Yes. Importing and tracing are free, use no credits, and need no account. Your first project is free whichever way you start it; a second project needs HomeDesigner Pro, which also adds PDF and DXF export.

Can I use a photo of a paper plan?

Yes, if it is a clear, straight-on photo or scan with even light. Photos taken at an angle give slanted walls. iPhone photos saved as HEIC need to be saved as JPG first.

My PDF has several pages. Which one is imported?

The first page. If your floor plan is on a later page, save that page as its own PDF or image and import that.

Are doors, windows and room names imported?

From a PDF or image, HomeDesigner imports the walls and builds the rooms; you add doors, windows and names in the editor. If you have the plan as a DXF (or DWG in the browser), import that instead: it brings in exact geometry with doors, windows and room names.

Is my plan sent to a server for tracing?

The plan is traced in your browser; it is not sent to a server for tracing.
