Before you buy

Good to know

Do I need an account?

For the web checkout, yes — sign in with Google so the purchase is tied to your account rather than one browser. On Android you can buy without signing in, but signing in is what carries Pro across to the web.

Does one purchase cover every device?

Yes, as long as you sign in with the same Google account. Pro bought on the web works in the Android app, and the other way round.

I already bought Pro

Open the app, go to the Pro sheet and choose Restore purchases, or simply sign in with the Google account you bought it on.

Refunds and billing

Web purchases are handled by RevenueCat and Stripe; Android purchases are handled by Google Play. Manage a purchase, or ask for a refund, wherever you bought it.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Drawing plans, furnishing a room and the 3D walkthrough are all free — try it in your browser before you buy.

Your data

Designs are stored on your device and, if you sign in, synced to your account. See the privacy policy.
