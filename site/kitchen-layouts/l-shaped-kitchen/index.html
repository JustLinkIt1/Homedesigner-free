L-shaped kitchen layout ideas, with example sizes

Example rooms: 3.6 × 3 m · 12 × 10 ft

An L-shaped kitchen uses two walls that meet in a corner and leaves the rest of the room open, which is why it suits kitchens that share space with a dining table. Put the sink on one leg and the stove on the other, so two people can work without bumping into each other. Open a room of this size and build each leg from free Base Cabinets: a row along the first wall, then a second row starting where the first one ends. The corner is the only tricky part. You can leave it as a plain worktop with two cabinets meeting, or use the dedicated Corner Cabinet (Pro). Check the corner in 3D before you decide.
