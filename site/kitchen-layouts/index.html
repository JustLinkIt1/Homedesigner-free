Kitchen layout ideas: small, L, galley, U and island

Most kitchens are one of five shapes. A small kitchen fits everything on one or two walls. An L uses two walls that meet in a corner. A galley puts two runs face to face. A U wraps three walls. An island adds a freestanding run in the middle. Pick a layout below to see example sizes, then open a room of that size in HomeDesigner. You place the units in 2D, line them up into runs, and walk around the result in 3D, all in your browser or on Android. Base cabinets, counters, the sink, stove and fridge are free.
