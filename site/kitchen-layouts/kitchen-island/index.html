Kitchen layout with island: ideas and example sizes

Example rooms: 4.2 × 4 m · 14 × 13 ft

An island adds a freestanding run in the middle of the kitchen: extra worktop, extra storage, and often a place to sit. It needs a bigger room than the other layouts, because you walk all the way around it. Many planners leave about 1 m on every side. Open a room of this size, build the wall run first, then make the island from free Base Cabinets placed back to back in the middle of the floor. That's a working island at no charge. If you'd rather drop in one ready-made piece, the dedicated Kitchen Island (180 × 90 cm) is Pro. Either way, walk around it in 3D and open the oven door in your head.
