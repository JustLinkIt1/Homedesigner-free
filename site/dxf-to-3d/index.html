DXF and DWG import

Your CAD drawing as a home you can walk through

HomeDesigner reads the drawing itself, not a picture of it, so walls come in as real geometry you can edit, furnish and view in 3D. It works in the browser and in the Android app, with no credits needed.

Real walls from your drawing

Choose a .dxf or .dwg file and HomeDesigner reads the geometry: lines, polylines, arcs and blocks. Before anything is placed, it shows how many walls it found and the scale it used.

  • Double-line walls: become single walls with their measured thickness
  • Units from the file: read and checked against a sensible building size
  • Clutter left out: dimension lines and stray title blocks are skipped

Doors, windows and room names come with it

Layer names tell HomeDesigner which lines are walls, doors and windows, in English, German, French, Italian or Spanish. Room labels written on the plan become the room names.

  • Doors and windows: from door and window layers and blocks
  • Room names: taken from the text on your plan
  • Storeys: named on layers are imported as stacked floors

Then see it in 3D

After the import you are in the 2D editor. Switch to 3D with one tap, walk through the rooms, and furnish them to scale before anything is built or bought.

  • Walk-through 3D: on your phone or in the browser
  • Edit after import: move walls, add openings, change rooms
  • Export: PNG for free, PDF and DXF export in HomeDesigner Pro

Questions

Good to know

Is importing a DXF free?

Yes. The import uses no credits and runs on your own device. Your first project is free whichever way you start it; a second project needs HomeDesigner Pro, which also adds PDF and DXF export.

Can I import a DWG file?

Yes. HomeDesigner converts a DWG to DXF in the browser before importing it. If a file can't be converted, save it as DXF from your CAD program and import that.

What in my drawing is imported?

Lines, polylines, arcs, circles and blocks, plus text for room names. Splines, hatches, dimension objects and 3D objects are left out: HomeDesigner builds a 2D plan and raises the walls to a standard height.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. The Android app has the same importer as the web app. Pick the file from your phone's files; in the browser you can also drag and drop it.
