The plan

Rooms and sizes

A small one-storey house with a tiled gable roof, 9 × 8 m (29.5 × 26.2 ft), 72 m² (775 sq ft). The front half is one open space: a 5.5 × 4.0 m (18.0 × 13.1 ft) living area beside a 3.5 × 4.0 m (11.5 × 13.1 ft) kitchen. Behind it, two bedrooms of the same size, 3.5 × 4.0 m (11.5 × 13.1 ft) each, sit either side of a short hall and a 2.0 × 2.5 m (6.6 × 8.2 ft) bathroom. Equal bedrooms make it work for a couple with a home office, two sharers, or downsizers who want a guest room. There's no wasted corridor, which is why it fits on a narrow plot.
