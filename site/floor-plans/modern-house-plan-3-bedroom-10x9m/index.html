The plan

Rooms and sizes

A modern two-storey house with a flat roof on a small 10 × 9 m (32.8 × 29.5 ft) footprint, 180 m² (1,938 sq ft) over both floors. Downstairs is built for daytime: a 6.0 × 5.5 m (19.7 × 18.0 ft) open living space and a 4.0 × 5.5 m (13.1 × 18.0 ft) kitchen across the front, with the stairs, a toilet, a storage room and a 4.0 × 3.5 m (13.1 × 11.5 ft) office behind. Upstairs, the largest bedroom is 4.5 × 5.5 m (14.8 × 18.0 ft), the second 3.5 × 5.5 m (11.5 × 18.0 ft) and the third 5.5 × 2.3 m (18.0 × 7.5 ft), with two bathrooms and a walk-in closet. It suits a family on a tight urban plot where one parent works from home.
