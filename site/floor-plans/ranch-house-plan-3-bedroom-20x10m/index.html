3-Bed Ranch House Plan with Garage

The plan

Rooms and sizes

A classic ranch: one long storey under a hipped shingle roof, 20 × 10 m (65.6 × 32.8 ft), 200 m² (2,153 sq ft). Along the front run a 6.0 × 5.0 m (19.7 × 16.4 ft) attached garage, a utility room, a 4.0 × 5.0 m (13.1 × 16.4 ft) kitchen and an 8.0 × 5.0 m (26.2 × 16.4 ft) open living space, so groceries go straight from the car to the kitchen. A hallway the full width of the house separates them from the bedrooms. At the back: the primary bedroom, 4.5 × 3.8 m (14.8 × 12.5 ft) with its ensuite, two 3.5 × 3.8 m (11.5 × 12.5 ft) bedrooms, a second bathroom and a storage room. No stairs anywhere, which suits families with small children and anyone planning to stay put as they get older.
