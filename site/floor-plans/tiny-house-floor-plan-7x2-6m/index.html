The plan

Rooms and sizes

A tiny house 7.2 m long and 2.6 m wide (23.6 × 8.5 ft), 18.7 m² (202 sq ft), under a single-slope metal roof. Everything sits in one row. At one end, a 2.4 m (7.9 ft) kitchen. In the middle, a 3.0 m (9.8 ft) living space that doubles as the sleeping area: a sofa bed or a fold-down bed goes here. At the far end, a 1.8 m (5.9 ft) bathroom, so it's away from the kitchen. There's no loft in this version. It suits one person or a couple who want to see what tiny living really feels like before they build or buy.
