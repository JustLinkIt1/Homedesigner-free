The plan

Rooms and sizes

A one-storey barndominium under a metal gable roof, 18 × 12 m (59.1 × 39.4 ft), 216 m² (2,325 sq ft). The front third is the working half of the house: a 6.0 × 5.0 m (19.7 × 16.4 ft) garage or shop, then an 8.0 × 5.0 m (26.2 × 16.4 ft) open living space and a 4.0 × 5.0 m (13.1 × 16.4 ft) kitchen. A long hallway with a utility room and storage keeps the mud and tools away from the bedrooms. At the back sit the primary bedroom, 4.5 × 5.5 m (14.8 × 18.0 ft) with its own ensuite, two 4.0 × 5.5 m (13.1 × 18.0 ft) bedrooms, a family bathroom and a 3.0 m (9.8 ft) wide office. It suits a family who works from home or needs a real workshop.
