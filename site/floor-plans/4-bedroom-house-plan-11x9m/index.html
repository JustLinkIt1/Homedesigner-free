The plan

Rooms and sizes

A two-storey family house with a tiled gable roof, 11 × 9 m (36.1 × 29.5 ft), 198 m² (2,131 sq ft) over both floors. The ground floor opens with a 6.0 × 5.0 m (19.7 × 16.4 ft) living space and a 5.0 × 5.0 m (16.4 × 16.4 ft) kitchen. Behind them are the stairs, a toilet, a utility room and the fourth bedroom, 4.0 × 2.8 m (13.1 × 9.2 ft), on the ground floor: useful for guests or a parent who'd rather skip the stairs. Upstairs are three bedrooms, 4.5 × 5.0 m (14.8 × 16.4 ft), 4.0 × 5.0 m (13.1 × 16.4 ft) and 4.0 × 2.8 m (13.1 × 9.2 ft), with two bathrooms. It suits a growing family on a standard plot.
