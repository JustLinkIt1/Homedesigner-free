The plan

Rooms and sizes

A one-storey 2-bedroom house with a tiled gable roof, 12 × 10 m (39.4 × 32.8 ft), 120 m² (1,292 sq ft) including the garage. The front row holds a 3.5 × 5.5 m (11.5 × 18.0 ft) single garage, a 3.5 × 5.5 m (11.5 × 18.0 ft) kitchen and a 5.0 × 5.5 m (16.4 × 18.0 ft) living space. A hallway with a utility room leads to the back, where there are two bedrooms, 4.0 × 3.3 m (13.1 × 10.8 ft) and 3.5 × 3.3 m (11.5 × 10.8 ft), a 2.5 × 3.3 m (8.2 × 10.8 ft) bathroom and a storage room. It suits a couple or a small family who want a garage without needing a bigger house.
