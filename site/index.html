Everything you need

One app, the whole journey

HomeDesigner takes you from a rough idea to a plan you can share — with the same project on your phone and in your browser.

Precise 2D plans

Smart snapping, live dimensions, photoreal floors and top-down furniture. Import an existing PDF or photo and trace it to scale.

Realistic 3D

Every wall and object appears instantly in 3D. Walk through doors, up the stairs, and adjust the sunlight to any time of day.

Real furniture

A growing library of quality 3D models — living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, office and lighting — with more added in the cloud.

Templates & projects

Start from beautiful sample homes or a blank canvas. Sign in with Google to keep plans and Pro across your devices.
