HomeDesigner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

The short version

HomeDesigner stores your work locally by default. If you choose Google Sign-In, your plans are also encrypted in transit and stored in our private cloud storage so you can open them on your other signed-in Android devices. We do not run ads or third-party analytics. We only send product emails if you switch them on. The app sends a small set of anonymous usage events, described under Anonymous usage events.

Community forum (website only)

The community forum at homedesignerapp.com/community is part of the website, not the mobile app. Reading it requires no account and no sign-in. Posting requires Google Sign-In, and creates a forum profile holding a handle, a display name, an optional short bio and, only if you choose to upload one, a profile picture. HomeDesigner does not automatically import or publish your Google profile photo.

Anything you post is public. Your posts, attached screenshots, your handle, your display name, your profile picture and your bio are visible to anyone on the internet and to search engines. Your email address and your Google account identifier are never shown to other users. We still do not run ads or third-party analytics — the forum sets no tracking cookies and embeds nothing from anyone else.

Posts you report are checked by an automated content classifier to help us triage them. It only sorts our moderation queue; it never removes anything on its own. A person makes every moderation decision.

Deleting your account deletes your forum profile, your discussions and your replies, your profile picture and post images, and your AI points balance (forfeited), along with your designs. In the app, open Settings, sign in, and choose Delete account. On the web, do the same at homedesignerapp.com/app/. Steps are on https://homedesignerapp.com/delete-account/, or email us (see Changes & contact).

Anonymous usage events

The app creates a random install identifier on first launch. It is not your Android ID, advertising ID or account. The app sends that identifier, the app version, how the app was installed (from Google Play's install referrer: campaign source and name, or "organic"), and nine fixed events: first open, template chosen, editor opened, 3D view opened, Pro screen shown, import succeeded or failed, plan exported (only the file format, such as PNG, PDF, DXF or IFC; never the file, its name or your plan), and Pro checkout step reached (only the step, such as sign-in, checkout opened, paid or cancelled; never your card, payment or account details), and Photo mode step (only whether the photorealistic or the standard photo started, fell back or was saved; never the picture or your plan). We do not store your IP address or user agent with them, and they are not linked to your Google account. These events cannot be switched off. You can reset the identifier by clearing the app's data. Events are kept for 13 months and then deleted automatically.

Data stored on your device

Projects you create are saved in the app's local storage on your device (or in your browser, for the web version). Files you export — project files, plan images, PDFs and renders — are written to the location you choose. Deleting the app (or clearing browser site data) deletes the local copy. Signed-in cloud copies remain available until you use Delete account in Settings, which also deletes your other account data (see Data retention and deletion), or ask us to delete them. How to delete: https://homedesignerapp.com/delete-account/.

Google Sign-In and plan sync

When you sign in, Google provides your stable account identifier and may provide your name, email address and profile image. The app uses the identifier to separate your private plan files from every other user's files. Plan snapshots, project names, thumbnails and deletion timestamps are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Google identity tokens are verified by our Cloudflare Worker and are not stored. Our server reads your email address only to check that it is verified (and, for card checkout, passes it to Stripe); it does not store it. Your account identifier is also the key for your points, purchases and AI render records. See the Cloudflare privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade can be bought in two places, and they involve different companies. In the Android app the purchase is processed by Google Play. On the web it is processed by Stripe (Stripe privacy policy) through a checkout page hosted by RevenueCat. We never see or store your card details in either case.

For Android purchases, our server verifies the purchase with Google Play and keeps our own record: the purchase token, order ID, product, purchase time and, if you are signed in, a link to your account. For web Pro purchases, a record is held by our billing provider, RevenueCat (RevenueCat privacy policy). We also query RevenueCat with a namespaced version of your Google account identifier to check web or older purchases, so Pro access follows you to your other devices. For point packs bought on Android, a one-way hash of your account identifier is passed to Google Play to tie the purchase to your account. None of this is used for advertising.

Product update emails (optional)

If you switch on Email me about new features and offers in the app, after sign-in or in Settings, we send occasional emails about new HomeDesigner features, AI point packs and offers, about once a month. This is opt-in only: the switch is never on by default, nothing in the app requires it, and signing in or buying Pro or AI points does not sign you up. We do not send these emails to anyone who has not switched it on. The emails go to the address Google provides when you sign in; our legal basis is your consent.

You can stop at any time and it is as easy as starting: switch it off in Settings, or use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email. One click is enough, no sign-in needed.

To be able to show that you agreed, we keep a record of each choice: your account identifier, your email address, whether you said yes or no, the date and time, the exact wording you saw and its language, and where you made the choice (after sign-in or in Settings). The app also counts how often the option is shown, accepted and declined, without your email address. Deleting your account deletes this record and your address.

We never sell, rent or share the list, and use it only to send these emails. Emails are sent from our own Google mailbox, nathan@homedesignerapp.com; no other mail service receives your address.

Optional AI design assistant

When you request room names, room styles or a project description, we send a limited summary to Cloudflare Workers AI: room dimensions and areas, furniture type names, door/window counts, and any project name or preference you choose to include. We do not send the full editable plan or its coordinates to the model. Your Google account is verified by our service to manage your point balance; your identity token and email are not included in the AI prompt. Suggestions can be inaccurate and should be reviewed before applying or sharing them. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

AI plan, designer review and voice tour. These tools use Anthropic's Claude models. For an AI plan we send your answers to the plan questions (storeys, bedrooms, bathrooms, size, furnished, garage), the description you type, and when you ask for a change, your request and the plan being changed. For a designer review we send a text summary of the current floor (room names, sizes, doors, windows and furniture types), an optional picture of the 2D plan (which includes any image you imported as a tracing background), and any focus you type. For a voice tour Claude writes a short narration from the same kind of summary, and that narration text is sent to ElevenLabs to be read aloud; the audio comes straight back to your device and we do not store it. We do not send your Google identity token, email or account details to Anthropic or ElevenLabs. Results can be inaccurate and should be checked before you rely on them. See Anthropic's privacy policy and ElevenLabs' privacy policy.

AI point packs are separate purchases from Pro. We keep private balance and transaction records to deliver features, prevent duplicate charges, and restore credits after failed requests. Google Play verifies Android purchases. On the web, point packs are paid by card through Stripe (Stripe privacy policy); we send Stripe your Google account identifier and email address to link the payment to your account. No automatic top-up is made.

Optional AI rendering

If you choose AI render and confirm the upload, we send a JPEG of your current 3D view and an interior-photography instruction to ElevenLabs, which generates the image with a Google Gemini image model. If ElevenLabs is unavailable, the same JPEG and instruction go to fal.ai instead. We do not send your editable plan file, Google identity token, email or account details to either service. The generated image is a visualisation and may change design details. Renders made on a free account at the lower price carry a small homedesignerapp.com watermark. See ElevenLabs' privacy policy and fal.ai's privacy policy for their handling of uploaded and generated media. The generated image is stored by ElevenLabs or fal.ai under their own retention terms, and we keep only a link to it. We do not control how long they keep it. Deleting your HomeDesigner account does not delete renders, or the 3D view images sent to make them, that ElevenLabs or fal.ai already hold: fal.ai keeps request inputs and outputs for 90 days by default, and ElevenLabs states no fixed period. Deleting your account removes only our link to them.

We keep private render job records, result links, point balances and transaction records to deliver results, prevent duplicate charges and restore failed-render credits. Account deletion removes the render result links and your account identifier from render records. A hashed allowance marker and pseudonymous usage records remain to prevent repeated free-trial claims; the marker is derived from your account identifier with a one-way hash. Points transaction records are kept as financial records, with your account identifier replaced by a one-way hash. These records are pseudonymous rather than anonymous: anyone who already knew your account identifier could recompute the hash, but the identifier cannot be read back from it. Images you save on Android are prepared in temporary app storage and shared to the destination you choose. Save an AI image you want to keep; provider links are not permanent storage.

Permissions

The Android app requests no dangerous permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access. It uses internet access (account sign-in, plan synchronisation, purchase verification, optional 3D furniture models, and AI assistance or rendering when you request it), plus the normal permissions for Google Play Billing, the Google Play install referrer, network state, vibration (haptics) and Google sign-in credentials.

Data retention and deletion

  • Synced designs (private cloud storage): kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Forum profile, posts and images: kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Points and purchase records: kept as financial records after deletion, with your account identifier replaced by a one-way hash (see above).
  • Anonymous usage events: deleted automatically after 13 months. They are not linked to your account, so they cannot be deleted per person.
  • Server logs: we keep only short-lived error logs from our hosting provider, Cloudflare. We have turned off its per-request logging, so request IP addresses and locations are not recorded in them. Their retention is set by Cloudflare.

To delete your account, open the app, go to Settings, sign in, and choose Delete account (or do the same on the web at homedesignerapp.com/app/). Full steps, and what is removed or kept: https://homedesignerapp.com/delete-account/. You can also email us.

Service providers

We use these companies to run HomeDesigner. Each processes data for us and only for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage, Workers AI and error logs.
  • Google: Sign-In, Google Play Billing and the Play install referrer.
  • RevenueCat: web and older Pro purchases.
  • Stripe: web card payments.
  • Anthropic: AI plan, designer review and voice-tour script.
  • ElevenLabs: voice tour audio and AI renders (using a Google Gemini image model).
  • fal.ai: fallback AI renders.

HomeDesigner is a general-audience app and is not directed to children.
