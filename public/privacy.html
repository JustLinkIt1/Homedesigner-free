HomeDesigner Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

HomeDesigner stores your work locally by default. If you choose Google Sign-In, your plans are also encrypted in transit and stored in our private cloud storage so you can open them on your other signed-in Android devices. We do not run ads or behavioural analytics.

Data stored on your device

Projects you create are saved in the app's local storage on your device (or in your browser, for the web version). Files you export — project files, plan images, PDFs and renders — are written to the location you choose. Deleting the app (or clearing browser site data) deletes the local copy. Signed-in cloud copies remain available until you use Delete cloud backups in Settings or ask us to delete them.

Google Sign-In and plan sync

When you sign in, Google provides your stable account identifier and may provide your name, email address and profile image. The app uses the identifier to separate your private plan files from every other user's files. Plan snapshots, project names, thumbnails and deletion timestamps are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Google identity tokens are verified by our Cloudflare Worker and are not stored. See the Cloudflare privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To recognise your purchase and restore it across devices, an purchase record is processed by our billing provider, RevenueCat (RevenueCat privacy policy). If you sign in, the purchase is linked to a namespaced version of your stable Google account identifier so Pro access follows you to another Android device. It is not used for advertising.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions beyond basic internet access, which is used only for account authentication, plan synchronisation, purchase verification and downloading optional 3D furniture models.

Children

HomeDesigner is a general-audience app and is not directed to children.
