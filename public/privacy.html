HomeDesigner Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

HomeDesigner works entirely on your device. Your floor plans, furniture layouts, imported plan images and settings are stored locally and never uploaded to us. We do not run accounts, ads, or analytics, and we do not collect personal information.

Data stored on your device

Projects you create are saved in the app's local storage on your device (or in your browser, for the web version). Files you export — project files, plan images, PDFs and renders — are written to the location you choose. Deleting the app (or clearing browser site data) deletes this data; we hold no copy.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To recognise your purchase and restore it across devices, an anonymous purchase token is processed by our billing provider, RevenueCat (RevenueCat privacy policy). This token is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions beyond basic internet access, which is used only for purchase verification and (on first use of some 3D furniture) downloading bundled model files shipped with the app.

Children

HomeDesigner is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.
